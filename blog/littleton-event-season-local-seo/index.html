Why events matter for search

Every event is a search spike in disguise

When Western Welcome Week fills the streets or the Candlelight Walk lights up downtown, people do not wander at random. They pull out their phones. "Restaurants near Main Street Littleton." "Parking downtown Littleton." "Coffee open now." The businesses that show up in those moments get the visits.

Most of those searches happen on the day or the day before. That means the work has to happen weeks earlier. A profile updated on event morning is often too late to matter.

The good news is that Littleton's event calendar is predictable. Plan once, reuse every year, and you build an advantage competitors who scramble each season never catch up to.

The big three

  • Western Welcome Week. Typically mid-August. The 98th ran August 7 to 16, 2026. 2027 will be the 99th.
  • Candlelight Walk. Downtown, the Friday after Thanksgiving.
  • Littleton Restaurant Week. Run by the Littleton Business Chamber. Check the Chamber for 2027 dates.

The foundation

Four things to set up once and reuse every year

An evergreen event page

One page per major event, such as "Western Welcome Week at [your business]." Update the year, hours and specials each season. The page builds history in search instead of starting over.

A special-hours habit

Google Business Profile lets you set hours for specific dates. Put every event date in your calendar with a reminder two weeks before to set them on Google, Apple and Bing.

A photo library

Photograph your storefront, staff and products during each event. Next year those photos become your profile posts, email images and social content.

A review rhythm

Event visitors are often first-timers. A small card or sign with a review link turns good experiences into fresh reviews right when your profile gets the most views.

Event by event

Western Welcome Week

As one of Littleton's longest-running traditions, Western Welcome Week brings residents, families and visitors from across the south metro. Searches shift toward food, drinks, family activities and parking.

Restaurants and cafes should make sure menus and hours are visible on their profiles. Retailers should highlight event-only offers in profile posts. Service businesses that are not downtown can still benefit by sponsoring, showing up and sharing photos, which builds local mentions and goodwill.

The Candlelight Walk

The Candlelight Walk marks the start of the holiday season downtown on the Friday after Thanksgiving. It is an evening event, so the key question for shoppers is simple: who is open late? Set special hours, post a profile update that says "Open late for the Candlelight Walk," and make sure your website says the same.

Littleton Restaurant Week

Run by the Littleton Business Chamber, Restaurant Week puts local dining in the spotlight. Check the Chamber for 2027 dates as soon as they are published. Participating restaurants should post their menu on their website as crawlable text, not just a PDF, and mention Restaurant Week in profile posts. For deeper help on menus, reservations and delivery listings, see this resource on restaurant marketing.

Not downtown? You still benefit

Businesses in Aspen Grove, near Southwest Plaza or out in Ken Caryl will not catch the foot traffic directly, but event weeks still change local behavior. Families out for the evening look for dinner on the way home. Visitors staying nearby search for coffee, gas and quick errands. A profile post that mentions the event and your distance from downtown, such as "ten minutes from Main Street, open until 9," can catch people heading home.

Light rail helps here too. Visitors who arrive at the Littleton·Mineral station pass Aspen Grove on the way in and out, which is worth mentioning in your posts if you are close by.

Paid search

When a short ad burst makes sense

Organic visibility takes time to build. Ads can fill the gap during the few days when demand spikes.

Search demand in the metro shifts with the seasons, and event weeks push it higher still. This look at seasonal search demand across the metro shows how much timing affects what people look for.

For event weeks, a focused Google Ads campaign can put you at the top of results for searches like "dinner near Main Street Littleton" while your organic presence keeps growing. Keep campaigns tight: a small radius around downtown, a short schedule, and ads that mention the event by name. Turn them off when the event ends.

Track what happens. Calls, direction requests and reservations tell you whether to repeat it next year. Our Google Ads management for Littleton businesses is built around exactly this kind of measured, short-run campaign.

After every event

The follow-up checklist most businesses skip

The week after an event is when the long-term value gets captured.

  1. Reply to every review within a week. Thank first-time visitors and invite them back.
  2. Add your best event photos to your profile. Fresh images help the next searcher picture the visit.
  3. Email new subscribers. Anyone who signed up during the event should hear from you while the memory is fresh.
  4. Note what worked. Busiest hours, best sellers, searches that brought people in. Add it to next year's plan.
  5. Reset your hours. Make sure special hours expired and regular hours are correct everywhere.

Bringing it together

Plan once, win every year

Event season is one of the few times when local demand is easy to predict. The businesses that do well are not the ones with the biggest budgets. They are the ones that updated their hours early, published a clear event page, asked for reviews at the right moment and followed up afterward.

Fresh reviews from event weeks are especially valuable. FAQ

Questions Littleton owners ask

When is Western Welcome Week in 2027?

Western Welcome Week typically runs in mid-August. The 98th edition ran August 7 to 16, 2026, so 2027 will be the 99th. Check the official event announcements for exact 2027 dates before printing anything or setting special hours.

Should I set special hours on Google for a one-night event like the Candlelight Walk?

Yes, if your hours actually change. Google Business Profile lets you set special hours for specific dates, which overrides your regular hours for that day only. Set them at least a week ahead so they show when people start planning.

How far ahead should I publish an event page on my website?

Four to six weeks is a good target for major events. That gives search engines time to find and index the page and gives your email list time to share it. Update the same page every year rather than creating a new one, so it keeps building history.

Is it worth running Google Ads only during event weeks?

For many downtown businesses, short targeted bursts make sense because search demand spikes around big events. Keep the budget focused on searches with clear intent near downtown, and turn the campaign off when the event ends. Track calls and visits so you know whether to repeat it.
