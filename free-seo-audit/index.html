    Tool 2 · Ten seconds

    Littleton Keyword Difficulty Estimator

    Type a search your customers might use, like "dentist Ken Caryl" or "how much does a new roof cost in Littleton." The estimator weighs the category, how specific the phrase is, local modifiers and buying signals, then gives you an illustrative score from 5 to 100.

    Use it to compare ideas, not to make final decisions. Short, broad terms in crowded categories score high. Longer phrases that name a neighborhood or ask a real question usually score lower, and they often bring customers who are ready to act.

    An illustrative estimate from patterns in local search, not live data. A real audit measures live competition.

    Reading your results

    What your grade means, and what to fix first

    The visibility grade weights each question by how much it tends to affect a Littleton business. A verified profile, the right category and consistent listings carry the most weight because nothing else works without them. Reviews come next, then your website, then the newer AI signals.

    A or B: your foundation is solid. The next gains usually come from better pages for each service and corridor, a faster review pace than your top competitor, and showing up in AI answers.

    C: you are visible but leaking. One or two gaps, often stale reviews or mismatched listings, are pulling you below businesses that are no better than you.

    D or F: customers who search for you by name can probably find you, but customers searching for what you do probably cannot. Start at the top of your gap list and work down.

    The gaps we see most in Littleton

    • An old address or phone number still live on Yelp, Bing or a data aggregator after a move
    • Apple Business Connect never claimed, so iPhone users see thin or wrong details
    • Reviews that stopped coming in after a busy season, like summer for home services
    • Columbine or Ken Caryl businesses listed under the wrong county
    • A robots.txt file that blocks AI crawlers by accident, often left over from a site build

    Most of these take an afternoon to fix once you know they are there. Finding them is the hard part.
