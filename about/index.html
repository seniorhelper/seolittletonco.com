Working together

What the first 90 days with us look like

Every engagement starts with listening. We want to know which jobs make you money, which corridors you want more of, how your phone gets answered and what has already been tried. A Columbine HVAC company and a Main Street boutique can both need SEO and still need almost nothing in common.

From there the work follows the same honest order: fix what is broken, build what is missing, then earn what makes Google and AI assistants trust you. You will always know which stage you are in.

  1. Weeks 1 to 2. Discovery, access set up in your name, and a full review of your listings, site and competitors.
  2. Weeks 3 to 6. Foundation fixes: profile, listings across both counties, technical issues and tracking.
  3. Weeks 7 to 12. New pages, review flow and AI readiness, with a monthly report tied to calls and forms.

What we won't do

The promises we refuse to make

Some things would make selling easier. We skip them anyway.

  • No ranking guarantees. Google and the AI platforms control their results. Anyone who guarantees a spot is guessing or gaming a keyword nobody searches.
  • No deliverable-count packages. We will not sell you a number of links or posts. We sell solving the problem that keeps you off the map.
  • No fake reviews or review filtering. We build an honest flow of reviews; see how in our Littleton review strategy.
  • No defense or government contractor accounts. We focus on local and growing businesses.

FAQ

Questions Littleton owners ask

Do you have an office in Littleton?

No. Our team works from Eye To Ad Media headquarters at 1001 Bannock St in Denver, about 20 minutes from Main Street. We would rather tell you that plainly than invent a local office, and most of the work happens on your listings and website anyway.

Who will actually work on my Littleton account?

The same small team that plans your strategy also does the work and reports on it. You get a direct contact who knows your business, not a rotating queue of account managers reading from a script.

Why don't you sell SEO packages with a set number of links or posts?

Because counting outputs rewards volume, not results. One well-built page for the right Littleton search can do more than ten generic posts. We scope work around the problem you need solved and report on calls, forms and bookings.

Are there businesses you will not take on?

Yes. We do not take defense or government contractor accounts, and we turn away work where we do not believe we can help. If another option fits you better, we will say so.
