Is my business in Littleton if my address says Littleton?

Not always. Only ZIP 80120 sits entirely inside city limits. Parts of 80121, 80122, 80123, 80127, 80128 and 80129 are in the city, and 80125, 80130, 80124 and 80126 are Littleton mailing addresses outside it. That matters for your service area settings and your local pages, and we set it up correctly.
