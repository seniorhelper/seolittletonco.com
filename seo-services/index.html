Technical SEO

If Google cannot load it, nothing else matters

Technical SEO is plumbing. Nobody admires it, but when it fails, everything upstairs gets wet. We check whether Google can find and index your important pages, whether duplicates or redirect chains are wasting its attention, whether your site works properly on phones and whether anything blocks crawlers by accident.

Most Littleton searches happen on phones, often on cell data in a parking lot at Aspen Grove or a truck cab in Columbine. A page that takes several seconds to show anything loses people before they read a word. That is where Core Web Vitals come in: three measurements Google uses to judge how a page actually feels.

The usual culprits on small business sites are oversized photos straight off a phone, a page builder loading scripts for features nobody uses, chat widgets and tracking tags stacked on every page, and cheap shared hosting. None of them are exotic. They just pile up over years of quick additions.

Site moves and redesigns are the other big risk. A new site launched without redirects from old page addresses can drop years of rankings in a week. If you are planning a rebuild, bring SEO in before launch, not after the calls slow down.

We fix what we can directly and write clear, prioritized tickets for your developer when changes need their hands. If you are curious what an SEO company actually does day to day, this is a big part of it.

Content

Pages that answer what Littleton actually asks

Good local content is not "Plumber Littleton CO" repeated 40 times. It answers the questions a real customer has before they call. How much does a sewer line camera inspection usually run? Do you work on the older homes near downtown with original plumbing? Can you get to Ken Caryl the same day?

We build service pages for the work you most want, write location content only where you truly have something specific to say, and add short, direct answers that both people and AI tools can lift. Every page has one clear job and one clear next step.

We skip the old trick of cloning one page across 30 nearby towns with the city name swapped. Those pages rarely rank for long, and they make a site look thin to Google and to the AI tools now reading it.

Content that tends to earn its keep

  • One strong page per core service
  • Honest cost and timeline guidance
  • Answers to the questions your front desk hears every week
  • Local proof: neighborhoods served, local photos, real details
  • Seasonal pieces tied to Littleton events and weather

Schema and authority

Label your facts. Earn your reputation.

Schema markup

Structured data tells search engines and AI tools exactly what each fact on your site is. We add the types that fit your business, which commonly include:

  • LocalBusiness or a specific subtype
  • Service
  • FAQPage
  • Organization
  • BreadcrumbList
  • Review snippets where allowed

Then we test it, because broken schema is worse than none.

Link earning, the local way

Links still matter, and the best ones for a Littleton business come from real involvement in Littleton:

  • Sponsoring a youth team, school event or community fundraiser
  • Membership in the Littleton Business Chamber or South Metro Denver Chamber
  • Taking part in Western Welcome Week or Littleton Restaurant Week
  • Real news: a new location, a milestone, a community project, pitched to local media
  • Supplier, partner and association listings you have earned

What we will not do: buy links, use private blog networks, trade links in bulk or post spam comments. They break Google's spam policies, and the businesses that rely on them tend to learn that the hard way.

Real local links do double duty. They tell Google you are part of the Littleton community, and they put your name in front of people who might hire you. A roofer who helps repair a community building after a hailstorm, then shares the story with local media, earns more than a link. A clinic that hosts a free screening at a chamber event gets a listing, photos and goodwill. We help you spot those moments, write them up and get them in front of the right people.

FAQ

SEO services, answered

What is technical SEO, and how do I know if my site needs it?

Technical SEO is the behind-the-scenes work that lets search engines crawl, understand and load your site quickly. Warning signs include pages missing from Google, slow loading on phones, duplicate pages and errors in Google Search Console. A short audit usually shows within a day whether it is a small cleanup or a bigger job.

What are Core Web Vitals in plain English?

They are three Google measurements of how a page feels to a real visitor: how fast the main content shows up, how quickly the page responds when you tap something and whether things jump around while loading. Google uses them as a light ranking signal, but they matter more for whether visitors stay and call.

Do Littleton Chamber memberships or sponsorships help SEO?

They can, when they are real. A chamber directory listing, a sponsor page for a youth team or a write-up about an event you supported are genuine local links and mentions that Google and AI tools notice. Join and sponsor because it helps your business and community; the SEO value follows.

Should I buy backlinks to rank faster?

No. Paid links that pass ranking value break Google's spam policies, and the cheap ones usually come from networks Google already discounts or penalizes. The risk is real and the upside is short-lived. Earned local links are slower and far more durable.

What should a monthly SEO report show me?

Leads first: calls, forms and bookings from search, and where they came from. Then rankings for the terms that drive those leads, map visibility across the city, and what work was done and what comes next. If a report is mostly impressions and charts with no tie to customers, ask why.

Can you work with my existing website and web developer?

Usually, yes. Most SEO work happens inside the site you already have, and we are glad to send clear, prioritized tickets to your developer. If the site truly cannot support what you need, we will tell you plainly and explain the options.
